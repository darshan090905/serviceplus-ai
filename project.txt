You are a senior full-stack frontend engineer, SaaS product designer, and UI/UX architect.

Build the complete frontend application for the following product.

# SERVICEPULSE

**Tagline:** Track. Analyze. Retain.

**Product:** Customer Retention & Service Intelligence Platform

ServicePulse is a professional B2B SaaS platform for service companies and AMC businesses. It helps service operations teams manage customers, AMC contracts, services, complaints, payments, customer health, renewal risk, and retention actions.

The most important product concept is:

```text
Customer Data
      ↓
Customer Health
      ↓
Renewal Risk
      ↓
Why is the customer at risk?
      ↓
Recommended Retention Action
      ↓
Owner Review / Approval
      ↓
Customer Follow-up
      ↓
Potential Renewal
```
